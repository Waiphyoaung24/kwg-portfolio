This supersedes the earlier sequencing restriction, not the evidence gates.

cost-profile and report bytes; require XAUUSD-VIP/M15 and an explicit UTC
dataset declaration. Raw broker epochs and synthetic gate-input conversion
must not be silently reused or shifted again. Compare the entire report with

remains unqualified, observed_days=0, promotion blocked, model_requests=0 and
simulator_provenance_unverified. UTC day aggregation of close-sampled curves
is accounting preparation, not proof of observed-day/session completeness.

null collection start/windows/holdout and explicit blockers. It starts no
process. Later freeze requires a future completed-bar start, ordered
development/validation and three flat-fold windows, separate untouched
holdout, dated clock/session/cost evidence and preserved complete diagnostics.

outside Git. Synthetic fixtures test this schema, not actual broker coverage.

Unsupported CLI report metadata or inconsistent identities must fail before
output creation. Exclusive outputs never overwrite prior attempts. Unknown
OAuth cost continues blocking gold dispatch; human promotion approval and
all risk/evaluation thresholds stay unchanged.
